Tzulun Huang

My research operates at the intersection of motion-based interaction in Virtual Reality, real-time computer vision pipelines, and expressive computational systems. I am interested in non-command interaction for spatial human-computer interaction.

To be more specific, my work explores computational systems that emulate the adaptive dynamics and motion trajectories of natural organisms. I design real-time sensing pipelines that capture movement trends and intent, translating body posture into low-latency, bi-directional feedback loops with predictive interaction models with spatial rendering engines. By replacing explicit command-driven inputs with real-time predictive tracking, these systems turn spatial environments and virtual worlds into continuous loops of interaction. For me, computational systems are the architectures that transform static boundaries into living and responsive canvases for human-machine co-creation.

Outside of work, I enjoy hiking and spending time outdoors—especially anywhere with trees!
