Tzulun Huang

My research operates at the intersection of motion-based interaction in Virtual Reality, real-time computer vision pipelines, and expressive computational systems. I am interested in non-command interaction for spatial human-computer interaction.

Specifically, I design interactive systems that take body movements into interfaces. My work explores computational systems at the intersection of functional tools and expressive mediums. For me, computational systems are the architecture that transform static boundaries into living and responsive interfaces for human-machine communication.

Outside of work, I enjoy hiking and spending time outdoors—especially anywhere with trees!
